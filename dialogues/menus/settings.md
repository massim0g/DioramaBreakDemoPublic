# display
Display

## windowScale
Window Scale
### windowScaleDescription
Sets the size of the window.
-
**Shortcut:** Bracket keys ('**\[**' and '**\]**')

## fullscreen
Fullscreen
### fullscreenDescription
Sets the window to borderless fullscreen. Automatically sets the window scale when toggled.
-
**Shortcut:** **F11** or **Alt+Enter**

## displayQuality
Quality
### displayQualityDescription
Affects certain graphically intensive visuals to improve performance on low-end hardware.
-
**Normal:** No changes. Recommended for most modern PCs.
-
**Reduced:** Slightly reduces fidelity of full-resolution graphics; reduces particle and geometry counts for certain procedural effects. Recommended for PCs with no dedicated graphics card or running on battery.
-
**Potato:** Greatly reduces fidelity of full-resolution graphics; greatly reduces particle and geometry counts for or outright disables certain procedural effects. Recommended for very low-end hardware.

### displayQualityEnum
Normal
Reduced
Potato

# game
Game

## dashMode
Dash Mode
### dashModeDescription
Determines how you control the player character's dash when outdoors.
-
**Hold:** Hold **Shift/X** to dash.
-
**Toggle:** Press **Shift/X** to toggle dashing.
-
**On by Default:** Player character dashes by default. Hold **Shift/X** to walk normally.

### dashModeEnum
Hold
Toggle
On by Default

## language

## dialogueAdvance
One-Button Advance
### dialogueAdvanceDescription
Makes it so you can also skip dialogue scroll with **Confirm**, not just **Cancel**, allowing you to both skip scroll and advance dialogue with only one button.

## dialogueSkip
Skippable
### dialogueSkipDescription
Enables the ability to rapidly skip dialogue by holding **Ctrl + Enter** or **RT + LT**. **Not recommended for first-time playthroughs.**
