# controls
## controlsKeyboardBasic
Movement - **WASD** or **Arrow Keys**
Interact - **Mouse L** or **Space** or **Z** or **K**
Dash - **Shift**
Pause Menu -  **Enter** or **Esc** or **Mouse R** or **X** or **J**
## controlsKeyboardDialogue
Advance Dialogue - **Mouse L** or **Space** or **Z** or **K**
Skip Scroll - **Mouse R** or **X** or **J** or **Shift**
Open Interjection - **Middle Mouse** or **Ctrl**
Open Log - **Alt**
Fast Skip (if enabled) - **Ctrl + Enter**
## controlsKeyboardCombat
Select unit/action/target - **Mouse L** or **Space** or **Z** or **K**
Undo Queued Action - **Mouse R** or **X** or **J**
Move Camera - **WASD** or **Arrow Keys**
Pin unit preview - **Middle Mouse** or **Ctrl**
Next Turn - **Tab**
## controlsGamepadBasic
Movement - **L Stick** or **D-Pad**
Interact - **A**
Dash - **X**
Pause Menu - **B** or **Start**
## controlsGamepadDialogue
Advance Dialogue - **A**
Skip Scroll - **X** or **B**
Open Interjection - **Y**
Open Log - **Select**
Fast Skip (if enabled) - **LT + RT**
## controlsGamepadCombat
Move Cursor - **L Stick** or **D-Pad**
Select unit/action/target - **A**
Deselect unit/action/target - **B**
Undo Queued Action - **LT**
Move Camera - **R Stick**
Pin unit preview - **Y**
Next Turn - **RT**
